Why blurting works

Close every book and set a timer. Write or say everything you remember about a topic without looking anything up, then check what you missed. This is retrieval practice: the act of pulling information out of memory strengthens it far more than re-reading ever does. The gaps you find are exactly where your revision should go next.

How to run a focused blitz

Pick one topic in critical decay or one you have never revised, ideally the one you would least like to be asked about in an exam. Spend the first ten minutes rebuilding it from scratch: diagrams, definitions, worked examples. Spend the rest attempting exam-style questions on it. Finishing the timer automatically marks your high-priority topics as freshly revised, so your retention badges reset.
